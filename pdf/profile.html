Who he is

About Babji
Neelam

Babji Neelam is the Founder & CEO of Technical Hub, a skill-development and incubation centre founded in 2016, and of Torii Minds, an AI-driven tech-education platform launched in 2025 and backed by NCC.

He began in August 1997 as a Lab Technician at APTECH, taught at Datapro, ICSS and NetGSC, and was faculty at the Aditya Degree and Aditya Engineering colleges. From February 2005 to January 2016 he worked in global IT: as a Java Software Engineer at IBM, then as a Project Manager at Wipro Technologies, on site in Australia and Switzerland.

His idea is simple: academic success has to translate into employability, so that every graduate steps into the workplace ready to contribute from day one.

16,000+Students certified

What he believes

Technical education should end in a job, not a certificate alone: mentored by working engineers, measured by industry certifications and proven on live projects.

The Four-Year Engineer

A B.Tech roadmap from semester 1-1 to 4-2: one programme for every year.

Year 1 · Foundations

Bamboo Coder

Roots first: programming logic, the first AI literacy and the habits of a coder.

Year 2 · Core CS & projects

SkillUp Coder

Core computer science, real projects and the first taste of industry.

Year 3 · Industry stacks & AI

Drive Ready · AI Ready Engineer

Industry stacks, AI engineering and placement readiness.

Year 4 · Production & placement

Owl Coder

Production AI, live campus projects and the placement run.

Moon Coder, from semester 2-2 to 3-1: extra coding hours that sharpen competitive-coding speed. AI Ready Engineer: a structured path from the first AI class to an AI engineer, delivered with Torii Minds.
