Professional Summary

Founder and Chief Executive with three decades across technical education, global IT and entrepreneurship. Began in 1997 as a lab technician at APTECH and spent seven years teaching technology; worked eleven years in global IT as a Java Software Engineer at IBM and a Project Manager at Wipro Technologies, on site in Australia and Switzerland. Founded Technical Hub in 2016, a skill-development and incubation centre bridging academia and industry, and launched Torii Minds in 2025, an AI-driven tech-education platform backed by NCC. Committed to one idea: academic success must translate into employability, so that every graduate is ready to contribute from day one.

“Talent was never the shortage. Industry readiness was. My mission is to bridge that gap.”
